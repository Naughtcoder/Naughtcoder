# Projects

Three iPhone apps I built, all local-first. Two use Expo and React Native, and one is native SwiftUI.

## [Household Expense Tracker](https://github.com/Naughtcoder/household-expense-tracker)
Tracks spending for a household. It reads bank texts and emails, files each payment into categories, splits shared costs, and shows budgets, trips and tax. Next.js website with a Supabase backend, plus a native iPhone app.

## [Healthy Me](https://github.com/Naughtcoder/healthy-me)
Follows a structured eating plan: meals, water, weight and supplements, with a sun-arc Today screen, a meal builder, Find a meal search, reintroduction days that fit new foods under a calorie ceiling, recipes read from a screenshot, voice and photo logging, Siri, widgets and Apple Health.

## [Photo Cleanup](https://github.com/Naughtcoder/photo-cleanup)
Finds screenshots, WhatsApp downloads, near-duplicates, selfies, documents and phone photos of screens, queues them for review with a category filter, and learns from what you keep and delete, including photos you teach it to always keep or delete. It runs entirely on the phone using Apple Vision, with no server and no paid API. Native SwiftUI.

All repositories use invented demo data only or no data at all.
